Project

Sera

Autonomous robotics · Precision agriculture · Computer vision

Overview

Sera is a next‑generation surveillance ecosystem that adapts to any scale—deploy a single unit for targeted monitoring or orchestrate a mesh network across entire industrial campuses, logistics hubs, and financial institutions for blanket coverage. Powered by advanced computer vision models running directly on low‑power embedded hardware, it continuously analyses its field of view, distinguishing between routine activity and genuine security threats with minimal false positives. Every frame is intelligently logged and time‑stamped, enabling seamless post‑event forensic visualisation and audit trails, while instant push notifications ensure security personnel are alerted the moment an intrusion is detected. Engineered for 24/7 reliability in demanding environments, Sera combines low energy consumption with robust edge AI to deliver enterprise‑grade protection without the complexity or cost of legacy surveillance infrastructure.
