Our Projects

Two flagship engineering challenges that showcase our expertise in embedded systems, AI, and robotics, built to solve real-world problems.

Code Name: Shinra

Real‑time environmental monitoring system built for industrial safety. Combines low‑power sensor fusion with edge AI to detect anomalies, which can lead to all sorts of harmful incident, before they escalate. Runs autonomously for a long time on a single battery in harsh environments,the main objective is to use it to anticipate the appearance of fire and to detect it in the case of emergency.

The project is a whole eco system with multiple cameras and robots at its center, the whole system works in harmony to anticipate, detect and fight all sorts of fire in factories with high risks of fire break out.

Code Name: Sera

Sera is an advanced AI‑powered surveillance system that operates as a standalone unit or scales into a fully synchronized network ideal for securing banks, factories, warehouses, and large industrial campuses. With real‑time computer vision and ultra‑low‑power embedded processing, it detects intruders, logs all activity for later visualisation, and instantly notifies security teams upon any breach.
